What dominates

At 377 reviews the import takes about 48.1 s. The process startup and imports are about 12.5 s; segmentation is 10.2 s, the e5 embedding and facet pass 11.8 s, the sentiment probe 6.8 s, and category classification 6.1 s. Normalize, aggregate and build-data are each under 0.2 s. A warm /api/data call is about 12 ms.

The 10-review batch is dominated by fixed model and import cost. The scaling signal is the 100 to 377 range; model load and process startup are effectively fixed.
